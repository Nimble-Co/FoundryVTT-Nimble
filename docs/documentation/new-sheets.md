# The New Sheets (Preview)

The new character sheet and the new NPC sheet are a preview. They are in the system beside the old sheets, and the old sheets stay the default. Try the new sheets, and tell us what you find. When the test period is over, the new sheets become the default.

## The new character sheet

The new character sheet has a rail with your Hit Points, mana, wounds and Hit Dice, and eight tabs: Core, Actions, Features, Inventory, Spells, Effects, Bio and Settings. A click on a row uses the thing. The arrow at the start of a row shows its full text. The pen at the end opens its window. Turn on edit mode to configure the values of your character and to delete items.

## The new NPC sheet

The new NPC sheet is for NPCs, minions and solo monsters. The rail has the Hit Points, the saves, armor, speed and the damage defenses, and four tabs: Abilities, Effects, Notes and Settings. The abilities are cards in columns, as a stat block, and their text starts open. A click on the name of an ability rolls it or posts it to chat. A click on a save rolls it.

Turn on **Edit** at the bottom of the rail (in the narrow form, the pen in the header) to change the monster: the portrait, the name, the maximum HP and the armor. An outline on the line under the name opens the monster window (level, creature type, size, monster type and damage defenses). Outlines on the saves and the speeds open their windows. In edit mode you can also create a feature and delete abilities.

### Open NPC sheets narrow

A GM opens many NPC sheets in an encounter. With the setting **Open NPC sheets narrow** on, the new NPC sheet opens 390 px wide, with the tabs in a strip under the name. Drag the edge of the window to make the sheet wide again. Each user sets it for their own screen, in **Configure Settings** on the Nimble tab. It is off at the start, and it changes only the new NPC sheet.

## Use a new sheet for one actor

1. Open the sheet of the character or the NPC.
2. In the window header, open the menu and click **Configure Sheet**.
3. In **This Sheet**, select **Character sheet (Hero)** for a character, or **NPC sheet (Monster)** for an NPC, a minion or a solo monster.
4. Click **Save**. The actor opens in the new sheet.

To go back, do the same steps and select **Nimble Player Character Sheet** or **Nimble NPC Sheet**.

## Make a new sheet the default for an actor type

Only a GM can do this.

1. Open the sheet of an actor of that type.
2. In the window header, open the menu and click **Configure Sheet**.
3. In **Default Sheet**, select **Character sheet (Hero)** or **NPC sheet (Monster)**.
4. Click **Save**. All actors of that type that have no sheet set by hand now open in the new sheet.

NPCs, minions and solo monsters are three actor types. Do these steps for each type.
