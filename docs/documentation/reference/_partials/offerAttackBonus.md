The Attack Bonus From a Row rule adds damage and a Primary Die bonus to an attack that a hero makes from the row of one item on a chat card. It adds nothing to another attack. With Feature Effects off, a row in Change This Roll adds them.

**Example: Juggernaut.** Your attack from a Coordinated Strike row deals extra damage equal to your armor, with +1 to the Primary Die.

- **Item with the row** → `coordinated-strike`
- **Extra damage** → `@attributes.armor.value`
- **Primary Die bonus** → `1`

The damage has the type of the attack.
