The Invoke Chaos rule adds a tick for 1 less mana to the spell dialog. The tick, or a crit on any attack, rolls on the Chaos Table. Each roll has its own card, and the system applies no effect of the table. With Feature Effects off, a button on the card makes the roll.

The world setting **Show Chaos Table rolls** picks who reads the text. With it off, the player sees only the number, and the GM gets the text in a whisper.
