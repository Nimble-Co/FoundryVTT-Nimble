**Example: Share My Pain.** The Zephyr's Swiftstrike can also target a 2nd creature within Reach 2.

- **Only attacks made through** → `burst-of-speed`
- **Extra targets** → `1`
- **Within Reach** → `2`

The Swiftstrike line on the Burst of Speed card then says "Up to 2 targets share one attack". Target both creatures and click **Attack**: one roll hits or misses each target against its own armor, and a crit is a crit on each. Echoed Agony adds one more with Reach 4, so the line says up to 3.
