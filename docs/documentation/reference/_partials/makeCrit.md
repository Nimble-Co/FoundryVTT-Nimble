The Make It a Crit rule puts a **Make crit** button on an attack card. A click makes the attack a crit: the Primary Die keeps its face and the crit dice are rolled. The crit stays for the card, so a later reroll or change alters only the damage dice. See [Making an attack a crit](../playing/dice-and-chat.md#making-an-attack-a-crit) for what the player sees.

**Who can use it?** is **The one who rolled**, **The target** or **An ally**. **The attack is** picks **Any result**, **A hit** or **A miss**. An attack that is already a crit has no button, and neither does an attack with 2 or more targets, an area attack, or a roll that cannot crit.

The other fields work as on the [Reroll](#reroll) rule: **Attack type**, **Only while**, **Only when the other creature is**, the cost fields and **Note on the card**.

**Example: Unleash the Beast.** 1/encounter, when you miss, you can crit instead.

- **Who can use it?** → **The one who rolled**
- **The attack is** → **A miss**
- **Charge pool** → `unleash-the-beast`

**Example: Bring the Pain.** 1/round, you may turn any melee attack against you into a crit.

- **Who can use it?** → **The target**
- **Attack type** → **Melee**
- **Charge pool** → `bring-the-pain`
