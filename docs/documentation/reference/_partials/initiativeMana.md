The Initiative Mana rule gives mana when its owner rolls Initiative. The dice show on the Initiative card, and the mana ends with the combat. With Feature Effects off, a button on the card gives the mana.

**Example: Elemental Surge.** Gain mana equal to your WIL when you roll Initiative.

- **Flat mana** → `@will`
- **Dice** → empty at level 1, `1d4` from a higher level

Use one rule per level range, with a condition on each.
