The Max Damage rule lets a creature take the max of a roll in place of rolling it. It is a tick in the roll window, or a choice per minion in a group attack. With **It is also a crit**, a max damage roll also counts as a crit.

**Example: Stormborn (2).** Spend 1 charge to deal max damage with a spell.

- **Roll** → `damage`
- **Charge pool** → `beastshift`
- **Charges per use** → `1`

A roll at max throws no dice, and a card with a max damage roll cannot be rerolled.
