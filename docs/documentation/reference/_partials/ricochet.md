**Example: Trickshot.** A hit with a thrown dagger ricochets to another creature within 2 spaces.

- **Weapon** → `dagger`
- **Weapon property** → `Thrown`
- **Within spaces** → `2`

After a hit with a dagger, the card shows the ricochet line. The player targets the other creature and clicks **Ricochet to** it; the GM applies half the damage, rounded down, through that creature's armor.
