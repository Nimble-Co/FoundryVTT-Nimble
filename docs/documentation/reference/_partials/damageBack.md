The Damage Back rule gives damage back to the one who attacked. When the owner takes damage from an attack, the attacker takes the same amount, ignoring armor. The amount is what the owner took after armor and reductions. A line on the attack card tells it.

**Only against crits** gives damage back only when the attack is a crit. **A Wound doubles it** puts a button on the card: the owner or the GM can suffer 1 Wound to deal the amount one more time, once for each line.

With **Feature Effects** off, no damage goes back by itself. The line has a **Deal** button.

**Example: Bring the Pain.** Whenever you are crit, reduce the damage by half. The attacker takes the same amount of damage you took (ignoring armor). You may suffer 1 Wound to double the damage the enemy takes.

- A **Damage Reduction** rule: **Mode** → `Half`, **Only against crits** → on
- A **Damage Back** rule: **Only against crits** → on, **A Wound doubles it** → on
