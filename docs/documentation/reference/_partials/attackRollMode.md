The Attack Roll Mode rule changes the roll mode of the owner's attacks. **Advantage** adds steps of advantage. **Ignore all disadvantage** takes out every disadvantage: Rushed Attacks, conditions, and what the target gives. The roll window opens with the change, and a roll with no window gets it too.

**Example: As One!** Attacks made with your Coordinated Strike! have advantage and ignore all disadvantage.

- **Advantage** → `1`
- **Ignore all disadvantage** → on
- **Only attacks made through** → `coordinated-strike`

On your second attack of the turn, the window opens at advantage, with no Rushed Attack line. Your other attacks do not change.

::: details Edge cases
- Disadvantage goes out first, then the advantage goes on. A window that starts at 1 disadvantage opens at 1 advantage.
- With **Ignore all disadvantage** on, a Held Change that ignores disadvantage is not used, so it stays for the next roll.
- Two rules add their advantage. One rule that ignores disadvantage is enough.
:::
