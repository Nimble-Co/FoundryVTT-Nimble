**Example: In the Cauldron.** A hazard. A creature in the boiling cauldron takes 1d20 fire damage each turn.

- An effect on the creature, with this rule on its **Rules** tab
- **Who takes it** → `The creature that has this`
- **When** → `Its turn starts`
- **Damage** → `1d20`, **Damage type** → `Fire`

At the start of each of the creature's turns, the active GM's client rolls and deals the damage. The turn start card gets the line, with **Undo**. When the effect ends, the damage stops.

**Example: Burning Aura.** Creatures that start their turn adjacent to the monster take 1d6 fire damage.

- On the monster's feature
- **Who takes it** → `Each creature within Reach`, **Reach** → `1`
- **Damage** → `1d6`, **Damage type** → `Fire`

Each creature next to the monster at the start of its own turn takes the damage, including the monster's allies. A dead monster deals no damage.
