The Table Roll rule rolls on its **Table**. The Chaos Table is the only table now. **When** tells what makes the roll. With the tick for 1 less mana or a crit, the spell dialog gets a tick for 1 less mana, and the tick or a crit on any attack makes the roll. With **When this item is used**, each use of the rule's item makes the roll, and the spell dialog gets no tick. Each roll has its own card, and the system applies no effect of the table. With Feature Effects off, a button on the card makes the roll.

The world setting **Show Chaos Table rolls** picks who reads the text. With it off, the player sees only the number, and the GM gets the text in a whisper.

**Example: Force of Chaos.** Invoke Chaos for 1 less mana, or on a crit.

- **Table** → Chaos Table
- **When** → The tick for 1 less mana on a spell, or a crit

**Example: Chaos Lash.** Invoke Chaos when you use it.

- **Table** → Chaos Table
- **When** → When this item is used
