The Crit Dice rule adds dice to a crit of the owner's attack, as a line of their own on the card. They do not explode. With **1/turn**, the rule adds them one time per turn, and a reroll that removes the crit gives the use back. With Feature Effects off, the rule is a row in Change This Roll.

**Example: Sneak Attack.** Your crits deal more dice, 1/turn.

- **Dice** → `1d6`, with one rule per level range
- **Name of the dice** → `sneakAttack`
- **1/turn** → on

A rule that changes dice of this role, such as a Change a Die rule with **A Sneak Attack die**, needs a roll that has them.
